Current situation

Hiring path today (SOP)

One People / Ops team. Candidate journey is mostly WhatsApp + manual follow-up.

1Apply
2Screen & book
3Interview
4More info / ID
5Airport permit
6Book test
7Pass test
8Verbal offer
9Day 1 on site

Junior / blue-collar reality

Candidates often need extra care — especially morning of day 1 (venue, supervisor contact, “I can’t find the gate”).

Current situation

Where the real pain is

Team feedback shifted the story away from “admins drowning in applications.”

Main pain

Hard to find candidates

  • Scarcity for junior airport catering roles
  • Bottleneck is attracting people into the funnel
  • Not primarily “too many applications to process”
Secondary pain

After hire, connecting is tedious

  • Offer follow-up and day-1 guidance stay human
  • When someone gets lost / doesn’t reply → phone call
  • This is care work, not form-filling work

Implication

A tool that mainly automates apply intake will not, by itself, fix the pains the team cares about most.

Options

Three paths

Option A

Keep current process

  • Personal / team WhatsApp as today
  • No new licence cost
  • Same chase and day-1 care load
Option B

WhatsApp Business app

  • Low / no platform fee
  • Greeting, away/OOO, quick replies
  • Labels + clearer SOP
  • Good enough for light volume
Option C

SleekFlow (~20k / year)

  • Shared inbox, Flow Builder
  • Apply bot + stages + broadcasts
  • Scheduled templates (Meta fees extra)
  • CTWA ads possible (ads budget extra)
Sense-check

What SleekFlow can and cannot do

Can help
  • Apply data capture when candidate writes in
  • Multi-person shared inbox & contact stages
  • Reminders / day-1 scheduled pings via templates
  • Re-activate old contacts; CTWA ad capture
Cannot replace
  • Finding candidates out of thin air
  • Interview / offer phone call
  • Live rescue when someone is lost on day 1
  • Airport authority permit / test process

Cost reality

~20k/year platform + Meta template fees for proactive outreach. Worth it only if message volume and multi-agent inbox pain are high enough to pay that back.

Recommendation

What I believe makes most sense

Choose Option B now — WhatsApp Business + clearer SOP

Our main problem is finding candidates and caring for them after hire — not processing a flood of applications. Paying ~20k/year for SleekFlow does not clearly buy relief on those pains today.

Do this next

  • Set greeting, away/OOO, quick replies
  • Use labels: Applied → Interview → Day 1
  • Write a short day-1 morning checklist
  • Track chats/day and missed day-1s for 1–2 cycles

Revisit SleekFlow if…

  • Several staff fight over the same chats daily
  • Apply / reminder volume becomes a real cost
  • We commit to CTWA / reactivation campaigns
  • We can show ~20k of time or lost-hire savings

SleekFlow is a strong messaging platform, but for our current pains it is the wrong spend — start with WhatsApp Business, measure, then decide.
