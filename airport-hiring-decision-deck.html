Extra care on day 1

These are junior roles. People often need a morning message with the meeting point, and sometimes a call if they cannot find the entrance.

What the team is actually struggling with

Not enough candidates

The hard part is getting people in. It is not that we are buried under applications.

Follow-up after hire is heavy

Reminders, day-1 guidance, chasing quiet replies — still done person by person on WhatsApp.

So any tool has to help with follow-up and keeping chats organised. It will not magically fill the pipeline by itself.

Options we looked at

1. Stay as we are

  • No new cost
  • Same phone / chat scramble
  • Nothing new to learn from

2. WhatsApp Business app

  • Cheap
  • Greeting, away message, quick replies
  • Fine for light auto-reply
  • Weak when several people share one inbox

3. SleekFlow

  • Shared inbox, bot, stages
  • Can schedule reminders
  • About 20k / year if paid yearly
  • They also offer 1–4 month contracts

Honest limits

SleekFlow can take on

  • Asking for name / phone / role when someone writes in
  • Keeping stages in one place
  • Sending planned reminders and day-1 pings
  • Letting more than one person handle the same chats

Still on the team

  • Finding people to apply in the first place
  • Interview and offer calls
  • Calling someone who is lost on the way
  • Airport permit / test with the authority

Also: if we message first after the free chat window closes, Meta may charge per template. That sits on top of the SleekFlow fee.

Where we landed

Start with SleekFlow on a short contract (1–4 months).

We compared staying put and WhatsApp Business. Both are cheaper up front. SleekFlow is the one that actually matches how we want to run this when hiring gets busier — shared inbox, apply chat, reminders — without signing a full year yet.

Use the trial for

  • Apply intake
  • Team inbox
  • Interview / test / day-1 reminders

Before we renew, check

  • Is chase / retyping actually down?
  • Are day-1 no-replies easier to catch?
  • Is the Meta message cost acceptable?
