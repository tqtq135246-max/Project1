Hiring path — airport catering

Updated after team pushback: the real pains are finding people, and staying connected after hire.

Pushback 1 · Real pain
Hard to find candidates
Blue-collar / junior airport catering roles are scarce. The bottleneck is attracting and keeping people in the funnel — not an admin pile of too many applications.
Not the main issue: “too many candidates for admins to process.”
Pushback 2 · Real pain
Post-hire connecting is still manual
SleekFlow bot mainly helps the application intake. After hiring (offer → day 1 venue care), the tedious chase / wake-up / hand-holding stays with the team on WhatsApp.
Bot does not remove the most tedious part of the journey.
