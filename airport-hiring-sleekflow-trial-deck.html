Pain points we are targeting

Pain A

Not enough candidates

  • Hard to attract junior airport catering people
  • When someone does write in, we cannot afford to drop them
  • Old applicants are easy to forget
Pain B

Follow-up is tedious

  • Booking, docs chase, test rebook — same chats again and again
  • Day-1 morning message + “I can’t find the gate”
  • Several people sharing phone chats gets messy

Not the main problem

We are not drowning under too many applications. The trial is about keeping scarce candidates moving, and cutting retyping / missed follow-ups.

How WhatsApp charging works (simple)

Often free / included in chat

  • Candidate writes first (apply)
  • Team / bot replies inside 24 hours
  • After they reply to a template, free chat opens again

Usually costs Meta fee

  • We reach out first after the window closed
  • Reminders, status, day-1 morning ping
  • Uses an approved Utility template (approx. US$0.03 per delivered msg to HK numbers)

Trial design: use the bot when they write in; budget Meta for the outreach we cannot avoid (remind / day 1).
