Part 2

What SleekFlow can offer

SleekFlow is useful when a candidate already writes in. The bot can collect application data and keep a contact record. Later messages the team starts may need Meta templates (extra cost).

Where it helps

  • Apply intake — QR / link, ask name, phone, role
  • Save contact + stage in one place
  • Some booking / reminder flows after the candidate replies
  • Shared inbox so the team sees the thread

How it usually works

  • Candidate writes first → bot can chat in the free window
  • Team reaches out later → often needs a paid template
  • When they reply → bot/team can continue again

Part 3

What is not addressed

Team pushback in one line: SleekFlow helps the application form — it does not fix the real pains.

1. Hard to find candidates

  • Main issue is scarcity — not “too many applications for admins”
  • Junior / blue-collar airport roles are hard to attract
  • A bot only talks to people who already showed up

2. After hire, connecting stays manual

  • Verbal offer call is still the team
  • Day-1 morning care is still the team
  • If they get lost / panic / don’t reply — someone must call
  • This is the tedious part after hiring

Takeaway for the decision SleekFlow can tidy the front door (apply). It does not create candidates, and it does not remove the post-hire connecting work that the team says is most tedious.
